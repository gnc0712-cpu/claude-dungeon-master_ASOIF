# Stormborn

## Setting
- **Era**: Original, unanchored — no fixed date relative to any named canon war or dynasty. Old enough that "the last war" is a thing veterans mutter about, not something the party will be asked to fight.
- **Season**: Long summer, waning — years of plenty, but the granaries have started filling a little slower and the maesters have started counting years on their fingers more than they used to.
- **Region**: The North
- **Campaign Frame**: Solo — a low-born wayfarer with no House, closest in spirit to "The Smallfolk Band" frame (see `dm-instructions/campaign-generation.md`), run as a single PC.

## House Rules on Record
- **Ability score stacking**: This campaign uses 2024-style backgrounds (ASI + origin feat come from Background). Culture of Origin (see `dm-instructions/character-sheets.md`) still grants its narrative traits and proficiencies, but does **not** additionally grant an ability score bonus when the character's background already provides one — avoids double-dipping between the two systems. Applies to all future characters in this campaign unless a player specifically wants to run 2014-style rules instead.
- **Non-SRD class content** (2024 Warlock features, any subclass not in `dnd-5e-srd/`): sourced from what the player provides, not from memory. See `dm-instructions/subclass-reflavoring.md`.
- **Warlock invocation progression (Ragnar only)**: 1 invocation known at creation (level 1) + 2 more at level 2 (3 total), rather than the SRD's 0 at level 1 / 2 at level 2. Player's build, applies to this character going forward.

## Recent Events
- Session 1: Campaign begins in Frostgate. Ragnar attempted (and pulled off cleanly) a pickpocket on a wool merchant in the evening market, right as an uncanny cold moment passed through the square — his dragonglass shard going ice-cold, torches briefly leaning away from him. A hedge-witch named Yarrow, who felt the same chill, approached him afterward and led him back to her house past the mill road. There she identified an Old Tongue word she'd greeted him with, "Sköll," as tied to old fireside tales of something ancient and cold that leaves nothing living behind it — except, this once, apparently Ragnar. She also reacted strongly on learning he was found north of the Wall in the worst storm the Night's Watch ever logged, and pointed him toward Castle Black's records as a real lead. Back in town, Ragnar recruited two founding partners for his planned mercenary company: Reyna Vask, a sellsword who'd watched him work the crowd, and Garrick Hollis, an axe-wielding sellsword in debt to a local lender. Both signed on as equal partners, not hired swords. The three of them closed the night with drinks at the inn and agreed to leave Frostgate for the Wall at first light.
