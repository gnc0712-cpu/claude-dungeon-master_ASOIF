# Blood and Coin

## Setting
- **Era**: The Long Truce — a generation ago, House Vantry rose against the ruling House Rosthane and lost. Rather than destroy the Vantrys outright, the peace was sealed with a marriage: Lady Wynafryd Rosthane to Ser Balon Vantry. The truce holds on parchment and at court; underneath, both sides still keep old grudges warm and compete for coin, information, and quiet leverage.
- **Season**: Long summer, waning — plenty on the surface, granaries filling a little slower than they used to.
- **Region**: King's Landing, the Crownlands
- **Campaign Frame**: Solo — an unsponsored, House-less pit fighter working King's Landing's illegal fighting-pit circuit. Closest in spirit to a custom "Pit Fighter" frame (criminal-adjacent, no formal company or patron), run as a single PC.

## House Rules on Record
- **Ability score stacking**: 2024-style background (ASI + Origin Feat from Background). Culture of Origin (Andal) grants its narrative traits and proficiencies only, not an additional ability bonus when the background already provides one — same no-double-dipping convention as the Stormborn campaign. Applies to all future characters in this campaign unless a player specifically wants 2014-style rules instead.
- **Non-SRD content sourced from the player, not memory**: the Pugilist class (Tavern Brawler, Fisticuffs, Iron Chin) and Piss and Vinegar subclass; the Tavern Brawler Origin Feat; the Fighting Chain martial weapon (2024-style Mastery property, "Disarming"); and the Grim Hollow-sourced background. See `campaigns/BloodAndCoin/characters/boscoe-waters.md` for the full text on record.
