# Blood and Coin

## Setting
- **Era**: The latter years of **King Robert Baratheon's** reign (confirmed session 1, player-established) — Robert's Rebellion is a generation past, the Iron Throne sits House Baratheon, and the long summer that's carried the realm since is finally starting to show its age. Canon events (Robert's Rebellion, the eventual War of the Five Kings, etc.) are background/legend, not something this campaign is walking through beat-for-beat — the party's story stays in King's Landing's underworld unless play explicitly turns toward the wider canon plot.
- **The Long Truce (local Crownlands subplot, invented)**: House Vantry backed the losing side (or stayed pointedly neutral) during Robert's Rebellion and paid for it in standing afterward; House Rosthane backed Robert's cause early and rose on the strength of it. A generation ago the two made an uneasy peace, sealed by Lady Wynafryd Rosthane's marriage to Ser Balon Vantry — holds on parchment and at court, but old grudges stay warm underneath, and both Houses compete quietly for coin, information, and leverage. Neither House holds the Iron Throne — that's House Baratheon's, full stop.
- **Season**: Long summer, waning — plenty on the surface, granaries filling a little slower than they used to.
- **Region**: King's Landing, the Crownlands
- **Campaign Frame**: Solo — an unsponsored, House-less pit fighter working King's Landing's illegal fighting-pit circuit. Closest in spirit to a custom "Pit Fighter" frame (criminal-adjacent, no formal company or patron), run as a single PC.
