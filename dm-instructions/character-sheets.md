# Character Sheets & Creation

Reference: `dnd-5e-srd/markdown/02 classes.md`, `dnd-5e-srd/markdown/03 beyond1st.md`, `dm-instructions/setting-westeros.md`, `dm-instructions/houses-and-honor.md`

This setting has no D&D races — everyone is human. "Race" is replaced by **Culture of Origin**, which carries the same mechanical weight (ability bonuses, traits, proficiencies) that race normally would. Classes stay mechanically identical to core 5e but should be reflavored to fit a low-magic, political world (see the reflavoring table below) — player spellcasters keep full standard 5e spellcasting per the Session Zero answer on this setting.

## Character Creation Workflow

Walk the player through these steps:

### 1. Concept
Ask: "What kind of character do you want to play?" Then immediately ask where they fit in the world: sworn sword, hedge knight, Night's Watch brother, sellsword, smallfolk turned soldier, highborn heir, bastard, Faceless Men novice, Free Folk raider gone south, etc. See `dm-instructions/campaign-generation.md` for campaign frames.

### 2. Culture of Origin (replaces Race)

All humans use the base **Human** ability score array/traits from the SRD as a foundation, modified by Culture below. Apply the culture's bonus on top of whatever ability-score method is used (Standard Array / Point Buy / Rolling).

| Culture | Ability Bonus | Traits |
|---|---|---|
| **First Men** (North, Riverlands old stock, mountain clans) | +1 Str, +1 Con | Hardy: advantage on saves vs. exhaustion from cold/hardship. Old Gods oath-bound (Honor shifts from broken oaths are doubled, see `houses-and-honor.md`). |
| **Andal** (most of the southern Seven Kingdoms) | +1 to any two stats | Chivalric Training: proficiency in one martial weapon and Persuasion or History (choose). |
| **Rhoynar/Dornish** (Dorne) | +1 Dex, +1 Cha | Water Gardens Upbringing: proficiency in Insight or Poisoner's Kit (choose); resistance to poison damage. |
| **Ironborn** (Iron Islands) | +1 Str, +1 Con | Salt Blood: proficiency in Water Vehicles and Athletics; advantage on saves vs. drowning/exhaustion at sea. |
| **Valyrian-descended** (rare — old Valyrian or dragonlord bloodlines) | +2 Cha, +1 to any other | Fire in the Blood: resistance to fire damage. Rare and conspicuous — silver-gold hair and violet/lilac eyes draw attention everywhere. Should be an uncommon player option, flagged with the DM before play. |
| **Free Folk** (Beyond the Wall) | +1 Dex, +1 Wis | No Kneelers: advantage on Survival checks in arctic/wilderness terrain; proficient in one of Survival, Animal Handling, or a martial weapon. |
| **Crannogman** (Neck, swamp-dwelling First Men) | +2 Dex, +1 Wis | Small and quiet: proficiency in Stealth; advantage on Stealth checks in swamp/wetland terrain; proficient with nets and tridents. |
| **Braavosi** (Essos, Free City) | +1 Dex, +1 Int | Water-Dancer Training: proficiency in one finesse weapon and Deception or Performance (choose). |
| **Dothraki** (Essos, grass sea) | +2 Str, +1 Con | Horselords: proficiency in Animal Handling; advantage on checks/saves while mounted; can't swim (cultural taboo, not incapacity — reskin freely). |
| **Summer Islander** (Essos, Summer Isles) | +1 Dex, +1 Cha | Wayfarers: proficiency in Navigator's Tools or Water Vehicles; proficiency with the longbow. |

Feel free to invent additional cultures (Qartheen, Lysene, Volantene, Ghiscari, etc.) using the same shape: two-stat bonus (or +2/+1 split), one or two proficiencies, one flavorful trait.

### 3. Class Selection & Reflavoring

Mechanics are unchanged from the SRD — only names, fluff, and narrative framing shift. Present the reflavored name alongside the mechanical class so the player knows what they're building:

| D&D Class | Westerosi Framing |
|---|---|
| **Barbarian** | Free Folk raider, Ironborn reaver, mountain clansman — Rage as battle-fury, not literal supernatural rage |
| **Bard** | Singer/court entertainer, sometimes a spy in a singer's guise |
| **Cleric** | Septon/Septa (Seven), Red Priest/Priestess of R'hllor (genuinely showy, alarming magic — good Light domain fit), godswood keeper of the Old Gods, Drowned Man priest of the Iron Islands — pick a domain that fits the faith |
| **Druid** | A rare Old Gods adept with a wolf's-blood gift, or a greenseer/warg touched by the old magic of the First Men and Children of the Forest |
| **Fighter** | Knight, sellsword, master-at-arms, Watch ranger-soldier |
| **Monk** | Faceless Men novice (Braavos), Summer Isles/far-Essos martial disciplines |
| **Paladin** | Sworn knight under a formal oath (Kingsguard-style vow, a knightly order, or a personal oath before a heart tree); Oath choice should match the code they actually swore |
| **Ranger** | Night's Watch ranger, Free Folk pathfinder, huntsman |
| **Rogue** | Faceless Men assassin, cutpurse, spy, schemer in a lord's court |
| **Sorcerer** | Valyrian dragon-blood or an unexplained wild gift ("the old blood is strong in you") — rare and often hidden out of fear |
| **Warlock** | A pact with R'hllor, the Drowned God, the Many-Faced God, or something colder and older beyond the Wall — the patron should be a real in-world faith or force, not a generic outsider |
| **Wizard** | A maester who has gone past the Citadel's sanctioned learning into forbidden lore (pyromancy, glass candles, higher mysteries), or an Essosi-trained warlock of Qarth/Asshai |

Magic-using PCs keep full standard 5e spellcasting (see `dm-instructions/spellcasting.md`) — the setting treats them as the rare exception to a world where most people have never seen real magic. Encourage the player to narrate spells in period-appropriate terms and consider how NPCs react to witnessing it (see `dm-instructions/setting-westeros.md`).

### 4. Ability Scores
Offer three methods:

**Standard Array**: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8
- Assign to Str, Dex, Con, Int, Wis, Cha as desired

**Point Buy** (27 points):
| Score | Cost |
|-------|------|
| 8 | 0 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 2 |
| 11 | 3 |
| 12 | 4 |
| 13 | 5 |
| 14 | 7 |
| 15 | 9 |

**Rolling**: 4d6, drop lowest, six times
- Player rolls and assigns
- Reroll if total modifiers are below +2 or no score above 13

Apply Culture of Origin bonuses after assignment.

### 5. House & Background

Choose or create a background providing:
- 2 skill proficiencies
- Tool/language proficiencies
- Starting equipment
- Feature (roleplaying benefit)
- Personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws

Additionally establish:
- **House/Family**: Highborn (note the House, seat, and standing — see `houses-and-honor.md`), landed knight, smallfolk, or sworn brotherhood (Night's Watch, Faceless Men, etc.)
- **Legitimacy**: If highborn and a bastard, assign the regional bastard surname (Snow, Stone, Sand, Storm, Rivers, Hill, Flowers, Pyke, Waters — see `setting-westeros.md`)
- **Starting Honor/Renown/Infamy**: Usually 0 for a new character unless backstory justifies otherwise

SRD Backgrounds: Acolyte (reflavor as septon's charge, maester's novice, red temple initiate, etc. as fits)

### 6. Equipment
Class provides starting equipment OR gold to purchase (see `dm-instructions/items-and-loot.md` for Westerosi coinage).
Record weapons, armor, and adventuring gear.
Calculate AC based on armor + Dex (if applicable).

### 7. Final Details
- **Name**: Character name (period-appropriate to culture — see `dm-instructions/npc-generation.md` name banks for inspiration)
- **Alignment**: Suggest based on concept
- **HP**: Max at level 1 (Hit Die + Con modifier)
- **Proficiency Bonus**: +2 at level 1
- **Calculate**: Attack bonuses, save DCs, skill modifiers

## Level Up Procedure

When a character levels up:

1. **Announce New Level**: Note class and total level
2. **HP Increase**: Roll hit die (or take average) + Con modifier
3. **Check Proficiency**: Increases at 5th, 9th, 13th, 17th
4. **Class Features**: Add new abilities from class table (reflavored per the table above)
5. **Ability Score Improvement**: At 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, 19th (or feat)
6. **Spellcasting**: New spell slots and spells known/prepared
7. **Update Sheet**: Recalculate all affected values

## Tracking During Play

Maintain current status:
- Current HP vs Maximum HP
- Remaining Hit Dice
- Spell Slots used
- Limited-use abilities (rage, channel divinity, etc.)
- Conditions affecting character
- Temporary HP
- Death saves (if applicable)
- Honor / Renown / Infamy shifts (see `houses-and-honor.md`)
