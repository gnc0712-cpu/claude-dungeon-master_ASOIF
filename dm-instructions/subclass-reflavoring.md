# Subclass Reflavoring

Reference: `dm-instructions/character-sheets.md` (base class reflavoring), `dm-instructions/setting-westeros.md`, `dm-instructions/spellcasting.md`

Flavor/reskin guidance for a full player-supplied subclass list, organized by base class. These are narrative reskins only — **not** a source of mechanical rules text.

## Before Using Any of These

- **Mechanics are unchanged.** A subclass's actual features, spells, and numbers work exactly as written in its source. Only the name, fiction, and in-world reaction change.
- **Non-SRD classes need the player to supply rules text.** Artificer, Blood Hunter, Gunslinger, Monster Hunter, and Pugilist (and most of these subclasses) are **not** in the bundled `dnd-5e-srd/` folder — several are official-but-non-SRD (Artificer) and several are third-party/homebrew (Blood Hunter, Gunslinger, Monster Hunter, Pugilist, and assorted subclasses below). Per the Rules Lookup Discipline in `CLAUDE.md`, don't adjudicate their exact mechanics from memory — ask the player to paste or summarize the official feature text and treat that as the source of truth for that character.
- **A few subclasses clash hard with the setting** (firearms, boxing-ring branding, vampires, elves) and need more than a rename to fit. Those are flagged individually below with a suggested bigger reskin — bring it to the player as a discussion, not a unilateral change, since it may affect how the mechanics read.
- **Rare should stay rare.** Anything marked "rare" below should be an uncommon, remarked-upon thing in the fiction — a character with it stands out, and NPCs should react accordingly (fear, superstition, opportunism), consistent with the low-magic framing in `dm-instructions/spellcasting.md`.

---

## Artificer

*Frame: Citadel-adjacent tinkers and smiths pushing past what's normally allowed — most of Westeros finds "artifice" indistinguishable from forbidden magic. Qohor (Essos) is the setting's other natural home for this class, famed for its smiths and sorcery.*

| Subclass | Westerosi Reskin |
|---|---|
| Alchemist | A maester (or Qohorik alchemist) working medicines, poisons, and Citadel-forbidden compounds |
| Armorer | A smith who's fused lost Valyrian techniques into living, wearable war-plate — rare and unsettling to see |
| Artillerist | A siege-engineer building scorpions and trebuchets enhanced with volatile, half-forbidden substances (wildfire-adjacent) |
| Battle Smith | A smith who's built and bound a steel companion — reads as constructed life, which the Faith would call abomination |
| Cartographer | A Citadel-trained mapmaker/navigator whose instruments and charts are uncannily, suspiciously precise |

## Blood Hunter *(non-SRD — see note above)*

*Frame: a grim, secretive order of monster-hunters — Night's Watch-adjacent or a hidden Essosi order — who pay a personal price to fight worse things.*

| Subclass | Westerosi Reskin |
|---|---|
| Ghostslayer | Hunts wights and the risen dead beyond the Wall |
| Lycan | Cursed with a beast-taint, hunts what cursed them |
| Mutant | Alchemically self-altered, Citadel-forbidden or Qohorik experimentation |
| Profane Soul | Bound to something dark in order to fight worse — a pact taken as a last resort |

This class's whole premise (self-harm for power, hunting monsters at personal cost) fits the setting's grimness well without much adjustment — lean into secrecy and the order's outsider status.

## Gunslinger *(non-SRD, and firearms don't exist in Westeros — needs a bigger reskin)*

**Flag this one for a real conversation with the player before use.** Firearms are anachronistic for ASOIAF; a literal "gunslinger" breaks the setting more than a rename can fix. Two honest options:
1. **Reskin the weapon, not just the name**: treat "guns" as a rare, exotic repeating crossbow or scorpion-pistol design out of Qohor or Asshai — mechanically unchanged, fictionally a marvel/threat wherever it's seen.
2. **Swap the chassis**: if the player's open to it, a Fighter (Arcane Archer or Battle Master) or Ranger reskin gets the same "precision shooter" fantasy without the anachronism.

| Subclass | Westerosi Reskin (if using option 1) |
|---|---|
| Deadeye | A marksman with the exotic repeating weapon, uncannily precise |
| High Roller | A gambler-duelist who's made the weapon part of their reputation |
| Secret Agent | A spy who's smuggled the weapon in from Essos |
| Spell Slinger | Ties the weapon to a caster's touch — treat as a rare Essosi hybrid tradition |
| Trick Shot | A showman-duelist, more performer than soldier |
| White Hat | A lawman/hedge-knight using the weapon to keep order |

|---|---|

## Pugilist *(non-SRD, and boxing-ring branding clashes with the setting — needs a bigger reskin)*

**The literal "boxing" framing (rounds, squared circle) doesn't fit Westeros, but the underlying fantasy — bare-knuckle pit fighter — has a genuinely good home**: the fighting pits of Meereen and Volantis in Essos are canonically brutal spectator combat. Reskin the whole class around that rather than a modern ring.

| Subclass | Westerosi Reskin |
|---|---|
| Dog & Hound | A pit fighter trained in a rough, working-class Essosi fighting school |
| Hand of Dread | A pit champion who fights under a fearsome mask/reputation |
| Piss and Vinegar | A scrappy underdog brawler, fights dirty and survives on grit |
| Squared Circle | Reskin the "ring" itself as the fighting pit's sand circle |
| Street Saint | A former pit fighter turned protector of their old neighborhood/slave quarter |
| Sweet Science | A technical, disciplined pit-fighting tradition — closer to a formal school |
